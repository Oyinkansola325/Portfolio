About Me

Healthcare, Evidence & Technology

I'm a Registered Nurse and early-career nurse researcher developing expertise at the intersection of healthcare, research, data, and technology.

My research niche is Digital Health. I am interested in understanding how technology, digital health systems, and healthcare data can contribute to better healthcare delivery, quality, equity, and outcomes.

My clinical background helps me approach healthcare data and research from a practical perspective. I care about not only what the data says, but what it means for patients, healthcare professionals, and health systems.

Alongside research and healthcare data, I enjoy medical and scientific communication and translating complex information into clear, useful content.
